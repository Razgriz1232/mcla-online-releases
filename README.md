# Midnight Club: Los Angeles Remix Online: releases

This repository holds the **releases** of the PC recompilation of *Midnight Club: Los Angeles Complete Edition* with its
online patch, and nothing else: no code, no game data. The launcher's installer and its updates come from here.

## Installing

1. Make a free account on [mclaonline.com](https://mclaonline.com) and open your account page: it shows your **game key**.
2. Download **`MCLA-Setup.exe`** from the [latest release](https://github.com/Razgriz1232/mcla-online-releases/releases/latest)
   and run it. It is **unsigned**, so Windows SmartScreen may stop it: click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. The setup wizard asks for a folder, your game key and the disc image of your own copy of the game
   (*Midnight Club: Los Angeles Complete Edition*, Xbox 360, USA/Europe). It builds the game on your PC from your
   disc, which needs the internet and about 10 GB of disk, and puts the key in the game's settings, so the game
   plays online from the start.

Updates come through the launcher: it shows **An update is available**, and **Update and rebuild** does the rest.

## The source

The game is built on your PC from its source, which comes with every install (it is in the install folder, as the
GPL-3.0 licence requires). The setup gets it from mclaonline.com with your game key. Each release's `NOTICES.txt`
lists the licences of everything in its files, and the SDK's source is attached to each release.
